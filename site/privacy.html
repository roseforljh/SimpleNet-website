Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026 · Operator: SimpleNet

1. Overview & Android VpnService Usage

SimpleNet ("we", "our") provides a VPN client application designed to establish encrypted network tunnels and route traffic reliably. On Android, SimpleNet uses the official Android VpnService API together with the sing-box core to create a device-level encrypted tunnel between your device and the selected VPN server.

SimpleNet does not require account registration, passwords, email sign-up, or phone numbers to use the service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum technical data needed to operate, secure, and improve connectivity:

  • Installation & App-Scoped Device Identifiers: A randomly generated local installationId and a one-way SHA-256 hash of the Android ID (scoped to the app signing key and Android user) sent to the SimpleNet dispatch gateway to bind the installation, issue signed node catalogues, and prevent abuse.
  • Region & Network Environment Signals: Before connecting, the app checks the device time zone, SIM/mobile network country and carrier indicators, basic network type (Wi-Fi or cellular), and verifies the direct IP country through our gateway to enforce regional access rules and select optimal routes.
  • Connection Diagnostics & Telemetry: Anonymous server latency tests, connection success or error codes, protocol used, session duration buckets, and app version sent to our gateway and Firebase to diagnose failures and improve node reliability.
  • Crash Reports & App Analytics: We use Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to collect crash stack traces and anonymous usage events. The Google Play distribution removes Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) permissions and does not use Advertising IDs or serve ads.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not log or monitor your browsing history, websites visited, DNS query payloads, or the contents of your VPN traffic.
  • We do not collect IMEI, hardware serial numbers, phone numbers, contacts, SMS, photos, or precise GPS location.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties or use VPN traffic for advertising monetization.

4. Third-Party Services

SimpleNet integrates Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics) for stability monitoring and anonymous usage analytics. Data handled by Firebase is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security & Retention

All API requests between SimpleNet and our backend servers are encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS. VPN traffic is encrypted between your device and the remote VPN server. Local node caches and credentials are stored using Android Keystore-backed encryption. Diagnostic and installation records are retained only as long as necessary for service operation, security, and aggregate reliability analysis.

6. Data Deletion & Your Rights

Because SimpleNet does not use user accounts, uninstalling the app removes all local configuration, rules, and cached data from your device. To request deletion of server-side installation binding or diagnostic records associated with your installation, email us at kunzel19912004@gmail.com and include the Device ID shown in the app's Settings screen.

7. Children's Privacy

SimpleNet is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will publish any updates on this page with a revised effective date. If you have questions or privacy requests, contact SimpleNet at kunzel19912004@gmail.com.
